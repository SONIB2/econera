# Construction Explorer

The existing homepage gave catalogue search priority and treated architecture as a separate illustration. The redesign makes the building the navigation surface. A responsive, original generated axonometric image uses native buttons at material coordinates and a parallel text control list. The panel uses the existing public catalogue projection with a bounded preview for each category. The two-dimensional approach avoids WebGL and preserves a touch and keyboard route.

The diagram depicts an illustrative house. Category examples are editorial examples. Live stock comes from approved listings with available quantity, and the availability form records a private buying need. It does not dispatch a supplier message.

This milestone delivers architectural navigation, database category integration, private availability requests and editorial public design. The catalogue reflects the selected category in its heading, and seller inventory adds a publication-state filter. The existing private profile and moderation workflows continue to operate. Material Passports with verified evidence, project workspaces, transfer-based impact measurement, and provider-backed AI remain planned.

Existing masonry, flooring, and door inventory keeps its category and ownership. The migration adds concrete, glass and roofing as selectable categories. Older windows listed under doors and windows remain discoverable in their original category.

## Material selection studio

The masonry hotspot links to the studio at `/materiale/murature`. Four URL-addressable selections swap an original architectural WebP, a material description, specification checks and filtered stock. Native links keep the selection shareable and keyboard accessible. Each selection loads one responsive image. WebGL would add rendering and asset costs without improving the distinction between these fixed visual choices.

The four masonry assets were generated for ECONERA with OpenAI image generation and encoded as WebP. They depict illustrative assemblies. Technical guidance describes what to verify for a lot and does not report tests or certifications for that lot.

Migration `202611170004_masonry_quotes.sql` adds the optional classification and seller-provided public label to the existing inventory and moderation workflow. Unclassified masonry continues to appear in the general catalogue. The studio shows published, approved stock with an explicit matching type. Public seller labels are optional. Account profile data stays private.

A signed-in buyer can submit a quantity, collection or delivery preference, and a message from a material detail page. The database resolves the seller from the current listing, checks availability and fulfillment, and deduplicates matching requests. The buyer and seller can read the request in their workspace. This first workflow covers submitting and receiving requests. Seller replies, accepted offers, stock reservation, orders, payments and logistics remain planned.

Flooring, roofing, windows and concrete retain their existing Explorer routes to real catalogue categories. Their dedicated visual selection studios remain planned.

The cutaway asset was generated for this application with OpenAI image generation, then encoded as WebP. It depicts an illustrative property. Preymaker's design write-up informed the image-led framing, while the architectural composition and artwork are original to ECONERA. [Preymaker design write-up](https://preymaker.com/insights-and-studio-news/preymaker-takes-flight-with-fresh-visual-identity). The Awwwards page timed out during review.

The homepage opens with a three-chapter story about salvaging and rebuilding. Its three original illustrative scenes were generated for ECONERA and encoded as WebP. Chapters work through scrolling and explicit links. Compatible browsers layer scroll-driven image motion over the chapter art, and reduced-motion preferences keep the scenes still.

The story ends in a conceptual CSS 3D pavilion. Visitors can change its material, roof profile and lighting, rotate it with drag or buttons, then copy a URL that reopens the model with those choices and the viewing angle. No account or database write is required for the concept. The chosen material links to the existing commerce experience. The cutaway remains accessible directly and continues to show live stock. The create-and-share finale draws on the structure of [Noomo ValenTime](https://noomoagency.com/insights/noomo-valentime-immersive-storytelling).
