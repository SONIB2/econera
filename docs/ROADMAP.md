# ECONERA roadmap

The showcase is the first localized slice. Continue Phase A by extending routing and dictionary coverage to World, accounts, catalogue, and Workspace. Prioritize seller offers, transactional stock reservations and orders before procurement and logistics expansion. Maintain the implemented or planned distinction in both showcase languages.

1. Establish the design system and catalogue experience.
2. Verify identity, local Supabase setup, and database authorization.
3. Complete inventory editing, media policies, and moderation.
4. Add organizations, business verification, quotation offers, atomic stock reservations, and offline orders.
5. Add reporting, operational telemetry, and provider-backed AI with read-only tools first.
6. Run live integration, mobile accessibility, operational recovery and launch checks.
